How to read support, confidence, and lift

Support is the share of baskets containing both sides of a rule. Confidence is how often the consequent appeared when the antecedent appeared. Lift compares that rate with the consequent’s overall rate. Lift above 1 means more frequent co-occurrence in this dataset, not a causal or healthy recommendation.

Data sources, mapping, and storage

Food names and category labels come from the provided grocery catalogue. Portion amounts and purchase frequency come from top_50_items_amount.xlsx. Nutrient estimates come from NZFOODNutrients.xlsx and are averaged across mapped FoodIDs where a food has multiple IDs.

Food groups are broad mappings from the supplied categories, aligned to the purpose of FAO/WHO GIFT nutrition-sensitive groupings. The supplied data does not include FoodEx2 codes, so this app does not claim official FoodEx2 item coding.

Nutrition intake.xlsx is shown as a project-provided reference table. Its values are separate from WHO guidance and are not personalized daily requirements.

After sign-in, your food log and personal rules are stored in the GreenHand Supabase Postgres database. Row-level security restricts records to your account. Without sign-in, temporary entries stay in memory in this browser session only.
